# The Salt Brothers, Part 1 — outline (about 28 minutes)

Logline: Two brothers in a dying salt town take in a wounded sand-wolf the size of a horse. The older brother wants to sell it to the warlord buying beasts for his war; the younger will not let it go. The town's water depends on which brother wins.

Title: Two Brothers Found a Sand-Wolf | The Salt Brothers | Full Fantasy Movie 4K
Thumbnail: a boy's hand on the muzzle of a huge pale wolf, white salt flats, riders on the ridge.
Debate question for the description and pinned comment: Was Taimur right to sell the wolf to save the town?

## Chapters
1. Cold open, 0:00–1:30. Night on the flats. The Beg's riders ambush a caravan for its beasts and its boys. A giant wolf fights, takes an arrow, vanishes into the dunes. Title.
2. The White Field, to 6:00. Kharsa at dawn. The brothers at the pans, the debt, the Beg's offer read in the square: silver for beasts, more for sons. Sohail shows he knows the tunnels better than Taimur. He finds the wolf dying in the Salt Cave and feeds it his bread. The bond is made without him knowing what it means.
3. Reg, to 11:00. Sohail hides and heals it; it grows strong fast. Taimur finds out and sees the end of the debt. Sohail refuses. Taimur: "when it is healed, we decide."
4. The Salt Road, to 15:30. Raiders hit Taimur's caravan; Reg saves it and the whole road sees. The Beg's terms arrive: the wolf, or the town's water is cut.
5. The Beg's Terms, to 20:00. Mirza and the town lean on Taimur. He sends Sohail to the far pans, chains Reg, takes the silver. Reg does not resist, because the boy once told it to trust his brother.
6. What You Feed, to 25:00. Sohail reaches the gate as the riders leave, is struck, and only then does Reg break the chains. No one dies; the Beg's men are wrecked. Jahan: "The wolf follows the boy. Take the boy." They ride off with Sohail; Reg follows into the desert. Taimur is left with the silver.
7. The Empty Pan, to 28:30. Taimur at the pan at dusk, Amma's one line, the silver thrown into the salt. He takes his father's knife and the camels. Last image: Qila Surkh on the horizon, wolf tracks beside hoofprints. Last line: "He bought a wolf. He does not know what he owes it." Card: Part 2.

Production: about 300 shots, ~30 hero shots with real motion, five speaking voices, realistic dark fantasy, salt-white dawns, amber lamplight, dust storms.
