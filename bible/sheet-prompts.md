# Character and location sheets: paste these into Nano Banana Pro

Save each result as `bible/characters/<name>/sheet.png` or `bible/locations/<name>.png`. Generate each one three times
and keep it only if the face and costume match across all three; if they drift, tighten the look string, never the prompt style.

Template, characters:

> Character reference sheet on a plain neutral grey background: three views of the same person side by side, front,
> three-quarter and profile, full body, identical face and costume in every view. Two small insets at the bottom: a
> close-up of the face calm, and the same face afraid. Photoreal cinematic dark fantasy film still, 35mm, natural light,
> fine film grain, muted desert palette. No text, no watermark, no cartoon, no modern clothing.
> Subject: [LOOK STRING]

1. Taimur: Taimur, 23, lean sun-darkened face, short black beard, thin scar through the left eyebrow, dark grey wool coat over a faded indigo kurta, salt-crusted leather boots, curved bone-handled knife at the belt, dust cloth at the neck
2. Sohail: Sohail, 14, thin boy, large dark eyes, cropped hair under a loose dust-coloured cap, oversized patched brown vest over a sand-coloured shirt, rope belt, salt burns on the forearms
3. Reg (replace "person" with "animal" and the insets with "head calm, head snarling"): Reg, a sand-wolf the size of a horse, coat white as salt with grey dust along the spine, amber eyes, torn left ear, arrow scar on the right shoulder
4. Qasir Beg: Qasir Beg, 50s, heavy calm warlord, trimmed grey beard, black quilted robe with red lining, silver rings
5. Jahan: Captain Jahan, 35, tall hawk-faced rider, black turban, red sash over lamellar leather armour, long spear
6. Amma: Amma, 50s, grey-brown shawl over her hair and shoulders, lined weathered face
7. Mirza: Mirza, 60s town elder, white beard, white cap, embroidered waistcoat over a long shirt, walking staff

Scale test (do this once): "Sohail standing beside Reg, the wolf's shoulder level with the boy's head, both facing camera, grey background" using both look strings. Keep it as `bible/characters/reg/scale.png`; it anchors every two-shot.
