# The Salt Brothers — series bible

Genre and tone: realistic dark fantasy, grounded and quiet. Wonder, loyalty, sacrifice. No romance, no nudity, no gore for its own sake, no sorcery used by heroes. Violence has consequences and is shown by its aftermath more than its act.

Theme: a wolf follows the one who fed it. So do brothers.

## World
Kharsa is a walled sandstone town at the edge of the white salt flats. Salt is its only living: raked from the pans at dawn, carried by camel along the Salt Road to the markets of the plain. The warlord Qasir Beg owns the only deep well and the road tax; a town that angers him goes thirsty. In the old stories, sand-wolves guarded the caravan roads until men began hunting them for war. Nobody under sixty has seen one.

The one rule: a sand-wolf bonds for life to whoever feeds it in its first hunger. It will follow that person to death. The Beg's captains know the rule; the town has forgotten it.

## Characters (use these names and looks exactly)
- **Taimur**, 23. Head of the family since the father died in the Beg's levy. Hard, responsible, afraid of losing what is left. Wants the debt gone and the town safe. Look: lean, sun-darkened face, short black beard, a thin scar through the left eyebrow, dark grey wool coat over a faded indigo kurta, salt-crusted leather boots, a curved knife with a bone handle at his belt, a dust cloth at the neck.
- **Sohail**, 14. Stubborn, kind, believes the old stories, knows the salt tunnels better than anyone. Wants to keep the wolf. Look: thin, large dark eyes, cropped hair under a loose dust-coloured cap, an oversized patched brown vest over a sand-coloured shirt, rope belt, salt burns on the forearms, a bread cloth tucked in the vest.
- **Reg**, the sand-wolf. Horse-sized, silent, moves low. Bonded to Sohail. Look: coat white as salt with grey dust along the spine, amber eyes, a torn left ear, an arrow scar on the right shoulder.
- **Qasir Beg**, 50s. Warlord of the plain. Never raises his voice; buys what he cannot take. Look: heavy, calm, trimmed grey beard, black quilted robe with red lining, silver rings, usually seated.
- **Captain Jahan**, 35. The Beg's recruiter and the face of the threat. Clever, watches before he speaks. Look: tall, hawk-faced, black turban, red sash over lamellar leather armour, long spear, rides a black horse.
- **Amma**, 50s. The brothers' mother. Few words, all of them weight. Look: grey-brown shawl over her hair and shoulders, lined face, sits by the hearth with a lamp.
- **Mirza**, 60s. Town elder who speaks for "everyone." Look: white beard, white cap, embroidered waistcoat over a long shirt, a walking staff.

## Locations (use these names exactly)
- **Kharsa**: flat-roofed sandstone town, one gate, lantern-lit lanes, salt dust on everything.
- **The White Field**: the salt pans at dawn, shallow water like mirrors, wooden rakes, white mounds, the brothers' two camels.
- **The Salt Cave**: old mine tunnels below the town, crystal walls that glow amber in lamplight, a hidden chamber Sohail knows.
- **The Salt Road**: the caravan track across the dunes, stone cairns, wind, bones of old carts.
- **Amma's House**: clay walls, a hearth, a hanging oil lamp, a curtain to the sleeping room.
- **Qila Surkh**: the Beg's red mud-brick fortress on the horizon, chained-beast pens inside (seen in Part 1 only from afar).

## Voices
Taimur low and controlled. Sohail young, quick, not childish. Reg makes no sound. The Beg soft and slow. Jahan clipped. Amma warm and tired. Mirza reedy and persuasive. Dialogue in English; Urdu and Hindi dubs later.

## Season arc (six parts, 25–30 minutes each)
1. The wolf is found, hidden, revealed, sold, and taken; Sohail taken with it; Taimur rides after them.
2. Taimur gets inside Qila Surkh.
3. The Beg's war and the chained beasts.
4. The brothers free the beasts.
5. The road home and the town's betrayal.
6. The Beg comes to Kharsa; the town finally stands.

## Episode formula
Cold open inside the wonder, title at 90 seconds, five or six chapters each ending on a turn, a reversal in the middle, a climax where the code is tested, an emotional resolution, a last line that demands the next part.
